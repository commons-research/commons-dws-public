Zenodo

Terms

  • Zenodo: General-purpose research repository developed by CERN and OpenAIRE for sharing research outputs.
  • OpenAIRE: European Open Science infrastructure supporting open access and research data.
  • InvenioRDM: Open-source research data management platform that powers Zenodo.

Purpose

Zenodo is a general-purpose repository designed to make research outputs easily shareable, citable, and preservable. It accepts datasets, software, publications, workflows, conference materials, and other research artefacts.

Relevance

  • Supports FAIR and Open Science practices.
  • Assigns persistent DOIs to research outputs.
  • Allows sharing of datasets, software, and other research artefacts.
  • Supports open, embargoed, restricted, and closed records during the research lifecycle.
  • Provides a REST API for integration into automated research workflows.
  • Metadata is openly available under CC0.
  • Built on open-source infrastructure (InvenioRDM).

Notes

  • Developed by CERN and OpenAIRE (launched in 2013).
  • Designed as a "catch-all" repository regardless of discipline or funding source.
  • Encourages reproducibility by preserving all digital research artefacts, not only publications.
  • Named after Zenodotus, the first librarian of the Library of Alexandria and an early pioneer of metadata.
