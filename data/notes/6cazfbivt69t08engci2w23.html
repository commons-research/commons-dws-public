Nanopublications: A Growing Resource of Provenance-Centric Scientific Linked Data

Nanopublications are broken into 3 main graphs: 1) assertion - formal statement 2) provenance 3) pub info Nanopubs are immutable and identified using URIs. Nanopublication indexes are used to retrieve grouped nanopubs (such as all data from a study). Nanopubs by their design focus on linked data.

The existing datasets analyzed that use nanopubs are highly heterogeneous and are largely provenance centric. With over 11 provenance triples per nanopub. SPARQL queries are able to do things like find common creators.
