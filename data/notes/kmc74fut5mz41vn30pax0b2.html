Sharing and community curation of mass spectrometry data with Global Natural Products Social Molecular Networking

There is a need for sharing and curating the datasets that are out there with NP MS data.

Mining and retrieval of datasets can be a real issue.

There are databases that exist for NP datasets. These are not necessarily freely available or process MS data. Existing MS/MS databases have limited analyses available.

GNPS: data-driven platform for storage, analysis, and dissemination of MS/MS spectra, allowing for sharing raw spectra, continuous annotation, and collaborative curation.

Use of MassIVE for public data set deposit and retreival.

GNPS spectraql libraries do dereplication of data, as well as identify spectra molecular networks.

User ratings appear to also be used to offer feedback on quality of datasets.

Submissions are peer reviewed, and corrections made as needed. Reannoation is iterative and done in a wiki-style fashion. This seems to mean there are records of changes present. Similar to the iterative annotation for the human genome project.

Dereplication using GNPS

Dereplication is done by comparing new MS spectra data against all accumulated reference spec in their libraries.

dereplication: analysis of spectra to identify isolated substances, allowing for finding interfering compounds and prioritizing extracts for chemical isolation.

then the second stage of dereplication they doing variable dereplication. Which is only available through their platform.

Accuracy can be rated by users directly for quality of the matches returned.
