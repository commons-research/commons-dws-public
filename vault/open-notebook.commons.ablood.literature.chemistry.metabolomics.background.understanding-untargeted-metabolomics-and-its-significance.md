# [Understanding Untargeted Metabolomics and Its Significance](https://www.evotec.com/sciencepool/understanding-untargeted-metabolomics-and-its-significance)
## Terms
- **untargeted metabolomics**: looks at the complete set of metabolites in a sample
- **targeted metabolomics**: looks only at the specific metabolites from the sample
## Advantages of untargeted
Allows for unique insights that may not be visible if only focusing on certain metabolites. Also allows for detecting metabolic shifts in what is being sampled. Such as from disease.
## Overcoming challenges in Metabolite Identification and Data Interpretation
Identifying unknown metabolites is challenging. There are many small molecules presetn in samples.
